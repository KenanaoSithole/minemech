The challenge

Reactive maintenance in mines drives high costs.

Most mines already collect the data they need — it just isn't structured to warn anyone before something breaks.

01

Fragmented condition data

Vibration, temperature and oil-analysis records sit outside the planning system — invisible to decision-makers when it matters most.

02

Unreliable CMMS history

Work orders exist, but inconsistent failure codes, weak asset hierarchy and missing context render historical data unfit for modelling.

03

Reactive execution cycle

Breakdowns, emergency overtime and urgent spares dominate operations — eroding margins, compromising safety and blocking planned production.

Without structure, data stays as evidence after failure — not intelligence before failure.
Who we are

How we work with a site

  1. Start with the data reality. Map what exists across sensors, historians, CMMS and manual logs before proposing anything.
  2. Clean the foundation. Fix the asset hierarchy and failure coding — the unglamorous work every model depends on.
  3. Build usable analytics. Baselines, alerts and health scores your team can act on from day one.
  4. Scale deliberately. Expand asset coverage and governance once the pilot proves out.
